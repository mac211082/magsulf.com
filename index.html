Applications

One molecule, six industries.

Magnesium sulphate is a genuine multi-market product. We supply each of these segments from the same documented source — consolidate your sourcing through one window.

01

Agriculture

Corrects magnesium and sulphur deficiency, drives chlorophyll and lifts yield in citrus, potato, banana, tea, palm and vegetables. Fully water-soluble.

02

Fertigation & Horticulture

Dissolves cleanly for drip, foliar and hydroponic feeding — no residue, blends with most nutrient programs for greenhouse and open-field growers.

03

Animal Feed

A magnesium supplement in livestock and poultry feed, supporting bone strength, muscle function, enzyme activity and healthy metabolism.

04

Industrial

Used across textiles, dyeing, detergents, water treatment, ceramics and as a drying agent — where consistent solubility and purity matter.

05

Pharma & Medical

BP/USP/IP/EP grades for laxatives, magnesium supplementation and formulations produced to pharmacopoeia specification.

06

Bath & Wellness

Clean, low-heavy-metal Epsom salt for bath soaks, muscle-recovery blends, scrubs, flotation pods and private-label wellness brands.

Buyer FAQ

Everything an importer asks first.

What is your minimum order quantity?
From 1 MT for samples and trial orders. A full 20-foot container holds 25–27 MT. We supply single containers or repeat multi-container programs.
Which grades and forms can you supply?
Heptahydrate (Epsom salt), monohydrate and anhydrous — in fertilizer/agro, bath & spa, food/FCC, pharma (BP/USP/IP/EP), feed and technical grades, screened to your required crystal size.
What are your payment terms?
TT advance or an irrevocable Letter of Credit (L/C) for larger and repeat orders. Terms are confirmed per order and per relationship.
Which port do you ship from, and where do you deliver?
We load at Tuticorin (V.O. Chidambaranar Port) in South India, with Chennai as an alternative, and ship worldwide on FOB, CFR or CIF terms to your destination port.
What is the typical lead time?
Usually 7–15 days from a confirmed purchase order and advance, depending on grade, quantity and packing.
Do you provide samples and documentation?
Yes — pre-shipment samples on request, and every shipment carries a batch-specific Certificate of Analysis, SDS/MSDS, packing list, Bill of Lading and Certificate of Origin.
Can you do private-label or custom packing?
Yes. Standard packing is 25/50 kg PP or HDPE bags and 1 MT jumbo bags; custom-printed retail packs are available with MOQ.
